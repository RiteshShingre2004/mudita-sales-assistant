Privacy policy

Lumen sales assistant is a prototype built for a hiring assignment. Lumen Analytics Co. is a fictional company. The prototype is operated by Ritesh Shingre (riteshshingre@gmail.com) and is only used with test accounts and people who have agreed to take part in testing.

What we collect

When you message the connected Instagram account, WhatsApp test number or email address, we store your messages, your name or handle as shown by that service, and your account identifier on that service (email address, WhatsApp number or Instagram-scoped ID). We also store what the assistant extracted from your messages (for example, the service you asked about and your stated budget), and a log of actions taken.

How we use it

Only to reply to your inquiry, decide whether it fits the fictional business's criteria, and arrange a meeting if you ask for one. Message text is sent to Groq, our language-model provider, to read it and word replies. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Data is kept only while the prototype is being evaluated, and no longer than 30 days.
