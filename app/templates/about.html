Lumen sales assistant

A prototype sales assistant built for a hiring assignment. Lumen Analytics Co. is a fictional company. The assistant reads test inquiries sent by email, WhatsApp and Instagram, asks follow-up questions, checks them against an editable business policy, assigns a sales rep and books a meeting on a test calendar.

It is operated by Ritesh Shingre (riteshshingre@gmail.com) and is used only with test accounts and people who have agreed to take part in testing.
